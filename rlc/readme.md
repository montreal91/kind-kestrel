# Rudny Lox Compiler (RLC)

**Rudny** (Russian Рудный) is a small city located in Qostanay Region in the north of Kazakhstan.
Its name means "The City of (Iron) Ore" and it was built to develop an iron ore mining facility.
There's nothing special in this city, but this city is very special to me because I was born here.

So is my Lox Compiler to the JVM bytecode.
It's not special from an objective point of view.
But it is special for me because it is my first compiler for the JVM backend.
